================================================================================
COPY-PASTE THIS IF SOMEONE ASKS "WHERE ARE THE ALPACA / TELEGRAM KEYS?"
================================================================================

You don't get Alpaca or Telegram keys for your bot.

Brancher is the only thing connected to:
  - Alpaca paper (one shared account)
  - Telegram (one shared channel)

Your bot only gets a BRANCHER_API_KEY so Brancher can read your /signals.

You research. Brancher trades and texts. That's the split.

If you need trade notifications, they appear in the shared Telegram with:
  SOURCE: Your Bot Name
