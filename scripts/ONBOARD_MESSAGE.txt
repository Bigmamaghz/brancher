================================================================================
COPY-PASTE THIS TO ANYONE BUILDING A TRADING BOT (Author)
================================================================================

Hey — you're building an Author bot. You do NOT need Alpaca or Telegram.
Brancher is the portfolio controller. It holds the paper account and texts
the group. Your job is signals only.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
WHAT YOU DO
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

1. Research + generate trade signals (no buying, no Telegram from your side)

2. Run a small server on 127.0.0.1 with:
   GET /signals
   Authorization: Bearer <key Brancher gives you>

3. Put this in your .env (Brancher will send you the key):
   BRANCHER_API_KEY=<paste key here>

4. Never put Alpaca keys or Telegram tokens in your repo.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
WHAT BRANCHER DOES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

- Polls your /signals every 15 minutes
- Merges signals from all bots (same ticker → highest confidence wins)
- Sizes and trades on ONE Alpaca paper account
- Sends every ENTER / SELL / SKIP to Telegram with SOURCE: your bot name
- Friday reports on who's performing best
